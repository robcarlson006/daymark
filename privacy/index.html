Daymark privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Daymark stores the budget information you enter on your iPhone. It does not require an account or send your financial records to an app-owned server.

Who operates Daymark

Daymark is operated by Robert McIlwrick Carlson, an individual developer. For privacy questions, contact robertmcilwrick+daymarkapp@gmail.com.

Information stored in the app

Daymark stores wallets, plans, expenses, income, refunds, savings activity, and settings in local app storage on your iPhone. The app has no account system, bank connection, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK in the audited release source. Daymark requests iOS complete file protection for its local financial files and excludes its local data file from device backups.

Backups and CSV exports

When you choose to create a backup or CSV export, Daymark writes a protected temporary file locally before you save or share it through iOS. The destination you select handles the exported file under its own terms and privacy practices. An exported copy may remain at that destination after you remove it from Daymark. Keep backups and exports private.

Exchange-rate requests

When an internet connection is available, Daymark may request a public daily exchange-rate table from Frankfurter at frankfurter.dev. The audited app request does not include selected wallet currencies, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, names, or record identifiers. Frankfurter and network providers may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address, request timing, and technical request metadata. Daymark caches the rates locally and calculates currency conversions on your iPhone. Converted amounts are estimates and may differ from a financial institution’s posted amount.

Retention and your choices

Budget information stays in Daymark’s local app storage until you remove the app or its data from your iPhone. Backups and CSV files you export remain wherever you choose to save them, and you manage those copies there. Daymark does not receive a copy of your financial records through an app-owned service.

Security

Daymark requests iOS complete file protection for local financial files. Protect access to your iPhone and store exported files securely. No storage method is risk-free.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Daymark’s data practices change. The effective date above will reflect the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, email robertmcilwrick+daymarkapp@gmail.com.
