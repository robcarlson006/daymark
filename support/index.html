Daymark support

Daymark is a personal budget app for iPhone. For help, email robertmcilwrick+daymarkapp@gmail.com.

Before you write

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue occurred. Please do not email account numbers, full transaction histories, or an unredacted backup unless specifically needed for your request.

Where is my budget stored?

Your budget records are stored locally in Daymark on your iPhone. Daymark does not have an account or a cloud sync service. The app excludes its data file from device backups, so create your own backup before changing devices or removing the app.

How do I back up or export?

In Daymark, open Settings → Backup & restore. Use “Create a backup” to save a JSON backup or “Export transactions as CSV” for a spreadsheet file. Keep exported files somewhere private. To bring a backup into Daymark, choose “Restore from backup” in the same section.

How do exchange rates work?

Daymark downloads a public daily reference-rate table when online, then calculates conversions on your iPhone. Cached rates can be used offline. These are estimates and may differ from card or bank settlement rates.
